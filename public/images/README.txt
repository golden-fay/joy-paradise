Real photography for the Joy Paradise website
==============================================

These files are already in place and wired into the site (Hero, About,
Mission and Contact sections). They are free-license photographs sourced
from Unsplash (no watermarks, no attribution required, free for commercial
use). Replace any of them at any time with your own authentic photography —
just keep the same filename, or update the src in the relevant component
under components/.

1) hero-companionship.jpg (1200x960) — Hero section
2) about-connection.jpg (1200x750) — About section
3) mission-community.jpg (1200x900) — Mission section
4) contact-support.jpg (800x600) — Contact section

If a file is ever removed or fails to load, the site automatically falls
back to a clean placeholder instead of showing a broken image.
